  [Shape 1]:
    - Conclusion
  [Shape 2]:
    - Our project provides an AI-powered platform for analyzing athlete movement from video.The system detects body movement, performs biomechanical analysis, and generates an injury risk score.It also provides personalized recommendations to help improve movement and reduce potential injury risks.The current system is designed as a working prototype with an explainable rule-based risk engine.In the future, we plan to use real labelled injury data, train an ML-based risk model, support more sports, and improve deployment.Overall, the project demonstrates an end-to-end approach to video-based sports injury risk assessment.
